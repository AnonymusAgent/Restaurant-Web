Our Story

A culinary journey since 2005

Founded in 2005 by Chef Pierre Dubois, La Maison began as a small Parisian bistro with a passion for authentic French cuisine. What started as a humble 30-seat restaurant has blossomed into a Michelin-starred establishment renowned for its innovative yet traditional approach to French gastronomy.

Our philosophy is simple: source the finest seasonal ingredients and let their natural flavors shine through meticulous preparation and time-honored techniques. Each dish tells a story of French culinary heritage while embracing contemporary creativity.

Meet Our Chef

Pierre Dubois

Executive Chef & Founder

With over 25 years of culinary experience, including training under legendary chefs in Lyon and Paris, Chef Dubois brings unparalleled expertise to every creation. His award-winning cuisine has been celebrated in numerous culinary publications worldwide.
