Privacy & cookies

A clear note about the small amount of data this demo site uses.

LAST UPDATED · NOVEMBER 2026

We keep it minimal.

La Maison is a fictional restaurant website concept. This notice describes the intended behavior of the site's first-party preferences and analytics when deployed. Replace this notice with reviewed business and legal details before production.

Essential preferences

An essential site_consent cookie remembers your choices for up to one year. It is needed to honor your privacy preferences. The display theme and accessibility settings are stored locally in your browser. These required preferences cannot be disabled in the site's controls.

Optional analytics

Analytics are off until you choose to enable them. If you opt in, a random first-party identifier is stored in site_analytics_id for up to 180 days, and the site records page paths without query strings, a referring domain, a broad device category and a timestamp. The identifier is keyed-hashed before storage. Raw IP addresses, names, email addresses, full referrer URLs and query strings are not written to the analytics table. Analytics records are scheduled for deletion after 90 days.

You can reject or withdraw analytics at any time using . Analytics data only includes opted-in visits, so it is not a count of every visitor.

Optional marketing

The marketing preference is off by default. If you opt in, this demo stores a first-party site_marketing preference marker; it does not send it to ad networks or load third-party advertising trackers. You can turn this preference off in the cookie settings.

Third-party services

The page templates may request fonts and icons from Google Fonts and Font Awesome; the gallery uses a LightGallery CDN, and the contact page embeds a Google Map. These providers may receive ordinary connection metadata such as your IP address when your browser loads those resources. Review their terms and self-host or remove external embeds if your production privacy requirements call for it.

Contact and production checklist

This concept currently displays a sample restaurant contact address. Before production, provide the real data controller, contact details, applicable legal basis and data request process. Reservation and contact forms use the visitor's email application and do not submit information to this website.

This page is implementation documentation, not legal advice. Have the deployed site's privacy and cookie disclosures reviewed for the jurisdictions where it operates.
